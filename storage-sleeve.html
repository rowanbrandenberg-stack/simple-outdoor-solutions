Common questions

Before you buy.

Does the sleeve close with a drawstring?

Yes — the sleeve does, with a cord lock. Note that the Outsak itself does not: that closes with a Velcro® top band, deliberately, so there is no cord for an animal to work at.

Will it fit an Outsak Spectrum?

It is specified for a rolled UL or UL micro. Nothing on the old catalogue claims Spectrum fitment.

Can I get it in a kit?

Yes — the Outsak UL Kit includes a bag, a liner and a matching sleeve for less than buying all three separately.
