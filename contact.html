Shipping & returns

Ships from Flagstaff, usually same day.

Everything is sewn in small batches here, so orders leave from Flagstaff — not from a warehouse three time zones away.

  • Free USPS Ground shipping on orders of $100 or more, before tax and after discounts.
  • Orders under $100 ship for a flat $4.
  • Priority (2–3 business days) is $14.99 for the whole order.
  • Most orders ship within 24 hours. We ship six days a week, and often the same day.
  • No international orders at this time.

Returns

  • 30 calendar days from your order confirmation email.
  • The product needs to be new, unused, undamaged and in sellable condition.
  • Get a Return Goods Authorization code from us before shipping anything back.
  • Return shipping is on you — but the gear is light, so it isn't much.
Warranty

We stand behind every stitch.

Each piece leaves one bench, checked by hand. If something fails in normal use, tell us and we'll make it right. Warranty length depends on the product:

  • Adventure Seat® — lifetime. No time limit, not ever.
  • Outsak® bags — three years from the date of purchase.
  • Harnesses, straps, liners, trail bags and sleeves — one year.
  • Escape Pod and Escape Pouch — 180 days.

Cover is for defects in stitching, materials and workmanship. Commercial use and modified gear are excluded. Repairs come before replacements where a repair is the better answer — that's how gear should work.

Wear from bears, rockfall, or a mule stepping on it isn't a defect, but write anyway. We've seen worse.

Quick answers

Asked often.

Which Outsak size do I need?

The Ultralight comes in 4.3 L and 7.5 L, for solo and weekend trips. The Spectrum runs 9 L to 25.7 L for groups and longer stretches. Full size chart on the Outsak page — or tell us the trip in the form above and we'll tell you straight.

Do you take custom orders?

Sometimes. If you need a size or configuration we don't list, describe it in a note. Small batches mean there's occasionally room to make one thing differently.

Do you sell wholesale?

We work with a small number of shops. Use the form above, pick “Wholesale or retail inquiry,” and tell us about your store.

Is the Outsak bear-proof?

No. It's built for rodents and small critters — mice, squirrels, marmots, ravens. Where a certified bear-resistant canister is required, carry one; our Bearikade Harness makes that easier.
