About the closure

The Outsak closes with a black hook-and-loop top band — genuine VELCRO®, guaranteed not to mould, mildew, or rot. Fold the top over, press it shut, done. There's no drawstring and no cord lock to fray, jam, or freeze up, and nothing for a rodent to work a tooth into.

Common questions

Before you buy.

Will it stop a bear?

No — and we won't pretend otherwise. The Outsak is designed for rodents and small animals: mice, squirrels, chipmunks, marmots, ravens. Those are what get into food on the overwhelming majority of trips. Where a certified bear-resistant canister is required, use one.

Does it close with a drawstring?

No. It closes with a black hook-and-loop top band — fold, press, done. There's no cord to fray or cord lock to break, and nothing loose for an animal to chew at.

Which size should I get?

Go by volume first. The Ultralight covers 4.3 L and 7.5 L for solo and weekend trips at 2.5–3.4 oz. The Spectrum runs 9, 12.15, 20.5, and 25.7 L for groups, base camps, and longer stretches between resupply. The full size chart has every dimension — or send us a note describing the trip.

Won't the mesh crush my food?

The mesh is flexible, so it forms around what's inside rather than forcing a shape on it. For powdered dinners, crumbs, and anything you'd rather not push through steel, add a Slap Bag liner.

How do I hang it or stash it?

In country without bears you can tuck it into a rock crevice or leave it under a tree. In bear country, hang it using proper hanging technique. One thing not to do: don't stake it to the ground — that gives an animal leverage to work against. And never store it, or anything scented, in your tent or near where you sleep.

How long does it last, and what's the warranty?

Every Outsak carries a three-year warranty. In practice the stainless mesh doesn't rot, rust out, or hold odour, so it tends to outlive the gear around it. Rinse it, let it dry, and fold it flat in a Storage Sleeve between trips.

Does it keep smells in?

No — mesh isn't an odour barrier, and it isn't meant to be. If you want scent control, put an odour-proof liner bag inside it. That's optional, not required.
