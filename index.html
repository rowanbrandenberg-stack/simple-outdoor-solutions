Where it began
Every piece is made by one pair of hands in Flagstaff. But it was never about the maker — it's about the canyon, and how much of it you get to see.

Decades of walking the Grand Canyon taught a simple thing: the less you carry, the farther you go, and the more you remember. Every Outsak, every strap, every cooler exists to hand you that — more trail, more quiet, more days out. Built by hand, one at a time, to outlast the trip.
