Common questions

Before you buy.

Is it waterproof?

Water resistant, not waterproof. Dyneema® shrugs off rain and damp ground; it isn't a dry bag rated for submersion.

Why is the Small listed as wider than the Medium?

Because that is what the old catalogue publishes — Small at 8 × 4.5 × 2.0 in and Medium at 8 × 3.5 × 2.25 in. It looks like a typo in the original listing, and we are checking it rather than quietly changing the numbers.

What do people use them for?

Kitchen kits, toiletries, first aid, electronics and cables, repair kits. Anything you want to find in one grab.
