Our story · Flagstaff, Arizona

Born in the Grand Canyon.

Where it began

It started with too much weight — and a long walk out of the canyon to think about it.

Decades ago, on the floor of the Grand Canyon, one truth got very heavy and very obvious: the more you carry, the less of the canyon you actually get to see. So the gear got lighter. Then it got smarter. Then other hikers started asking where they could get some. That's the whole origin — no boardroom, no brand launch. Just a better way to carry less.

Made by one pair of hands

Not a factory. A workbench.

Every Outsak, every strap, every cooler is cut, sewn, and finished by hand in Flagstaff — a few at a time. Stainless mesh, webbing, a sewing machine, and a lot of attention. It was never about the person at the bench. It's about what that care lets you do out there.

Small batches

Made a handful at a time.

We build in small runs so we can stand behind every stitch. Each Outsak leaves the bench with its label sewn on and its hook-and-loop closure checked by hand — the same way it's been done since 2008.

The rule

Minimal means every ounce earns its place.

01 Nothing extra

If a component doesn't make the gear lighter, tougher, or more useful, it doesn't ship.

02 Built to last

Stainless steel mesh, real webbing, honest stitching — made to outlast the trip, and the next one.

03 Field-tested

We use what we make, down in the Grand Canyon and across the Colorado Plateau, all the time.

The whole kit

One bag became a whole system.

What started as a single animal-proof food bag is now a full range — food protection, insulated coolers and cozies, bottle straps, sit pads — each new piece held to the same rule as the first. Seventeen years of small batches, still made by hand.

Still about the canyon

Carry less. Go deeper.

Everything we make exists to give you more trail, more quiet, and more days out there.
